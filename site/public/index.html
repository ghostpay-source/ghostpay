How it works

Stealth addresses, ERC-5564. No mixer, no pool, no custody. The contract only forwards money and posts a short note.

  1. Sign once

    Your wallet signs one message. A spending key and a viewing key are derived from it in your browser and never leave it.

  2. Claim a name

    The public half of your keys goes on chain under a name. ghostpayfin.xyz/pay/yourname is your link.

  3. A new address per payment

    The payer's browser mixes your public keys with fresh randomness. The money goes to the result in the same transaction.

  4. Your key finds it

    Your inbox reads every note, recognises yours with a one-byte view tag and rebuilds the key of each address. Collect to any wallet.

Questions

Who holds the money?

You. Each payment sits on its own address and only your keys can move it. The contract has no owner and keeps nothing between calls.

What can people see?

That some address received some amount at some time. Not that it was you, and not that two payments went to the same person.

Where do my keys live?

In the open tab only. Signing the same message again, on any device, gives the same keys back.

What does it cost?

No fee. Robinhood Chain gas, a fraction of a cent.

Is collecting private too?

Collect to a fresh wallet to keep it private. Collecting to your main wallet links that payment to it.

Audited?

No. The contract is about a hundred lines with no owner. Read it on GitHub.
