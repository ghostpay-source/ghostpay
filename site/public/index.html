How it works

Stealth addresses, ERC-5564. No mixer, no pool, no custody.

i

Sign once, get two keys

Your wallet signs one message. A spending key and a viewing key are derived from it in your browser. Their public half is your link, under a name you claim.

ii

Every payment gets a new address

The payer's browser mixes your public keys with fresh randomness. The contract sends the money to the result and posts a short public note.

iii

Your viewing key finds it

Your inbox reads the notes, recognises yours and rebuilds each address key. Collect to any wallet when you want.

Get paid in stocks

Every token payment carries 0.00002 ETH so the address can pay its own gas later.

Questions

Who holds the money?

You. Each payment sits on its own address and only your keys can move it. The contract has no owner and keeps nothing.

What can people see?

That some address received some amount. Not that it was you, and not that two payments went to the same person.

Where do my keys live?

In the open tab only. Signing the same message again, on any device, gives the same keys.

What does it cost?

No fee. Robinhood Chain gas, a fraction of a cent.

Is collecting private too?

Collect to a fresh wallet to keep it private. Collecting to your main wallet links that payment to it.

Audited?

No. The contract is about a hundred lines with no owner; read it in the docs.
